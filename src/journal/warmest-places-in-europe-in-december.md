**Short answer:** of the 39 European weather stations on our sheets that print a December average high, the warmest is **Larnaka, on the south coast of Cyprus, at 18.9 °C**. Among the places on the western side of the Mediterranean, **Palma de Mallorca, at 17.5 °C**, leads, with [Valencia](/spain/valencia/) (17.3 °C) and [Seville](/spain/seville/) (16.8 °C) close behind, then [Lisbon](/portugal/lisbon/) (15.4 °C) and [Athens](/greece/athens/) (15.1 °C). Spain holds four of the top seven places. None of them is beach weather: no European station on our sheets reaches the 20 °C average high our [December list](/best-places-to-visit-in-december/) asks for.

That is a ranking of the stations we cover, not of every town in Europe. Every number below is printed on one of our destination sheets, which name the station and the source, or comes from the same official station file. Where a figure has a caveat, the caveat is in the text.

## How we ranked them

We took the December row from the climate table on every European sheet: the average daily high, the average daily low, and the December rain total. Most of the tables are World Meteorological Organization 1991–2020 normals distributed by NOAA NCEI. Lisbon's comes from Portugal's weather service (IPMA), and the UK cities from the Met Office, both 1991–2020. **Athens is the exception:** its table is the Hellenic National Meteorological Service's Elliniko summary for 1955–2010, an older and longer period. We keep it in the ranking and flag it.

Where a country sheet and a city sheet share a station (Madrid and the Spain sheet, Rome and the Italy sheet), the station is counted once. [Santorini](/greece/santorini/) prints no table and [Dubrovnik](/croatia/dubrovnik/) prints rain only (138.8 mm in December), so neither can be ranked.

*Updated 25 November 2026.* [Cyprus](/cyprus/) got its own sheet on 25 November, so Larnaka Airport (WMO 17609, 2 m) is now in the ranking, and it goes straight to the top. Everything else moves down one place; no other figure changed.

The Italian rows print two decimals because the source files do; we copy them as printed.

Three things jump out.

**Larnaka is 1.4 °C clear of the field by day, but not by night.** Its December high of 18.9 °C is the best in the table. Its December low, 9.7 °C, is second to Palma's, and its 95.4 mm of rain is more than Palma, Valencia and Seville get. Larnaka is the airport station on the south coast; inland Nicosia and the Troodos mountains are not this table.

**The Spanish three are within 0.7 °C of each other.** Palma, Valencia and Seville are effectively tied on daytime warmth. What separates them is the night and the rain. Palma's average December low is 10.1 °C, the mildest of all 39 stations; the station is at the port, 3 m up. Seville's is 7.4 °C, 2.7 °C cooler.

**Lisbon is the wettest of the warm group.** Its December high of 15.4 °C comes with 108.5 mm of rain, the only top-six station over 100 mm. It also has the third-mildest nights (9.4 °C), behind Palma and Larnaka, so a Lisbon December is mild and damp rather than cold.

**Madrid is not a winter-sun city.** It is in Spain, but the Retiro station sits at 667 m, and its December high of 10.4 °C is closer to Florence than to Valencia. If "Spain in December" means warmth, the tables point to the coast and to Seville, not the capital.

The rain-day counts come from the same WMO station files our sheets cite, read on 24 November 2026; Lisbon's and Athens's sheets use other sources, and we did not open a rain-day figure for them. The Larnaka file prints 25.8 days with at least 1 mm in December and 123.8 a year against an annual total of 350.1 mm; we could not reconcile those, so we leave Larnaka's rain days out.

**Valencia is the best all-rounder in the table.** It is third on warmth, second-driest of the top eleven, and has rain on about four days in an average December. Barcelona is drier still, but it is 2.6 °C cooler by day. Podgorica is the cautionary tale: 11.8 °C sounds mild for the Balkans, but its 231.8 mm makes it the wettest December station on any of our European sheets.

## Sunshine: Seville wins the light

Several of the station files also print the average total hours of sunshine for each month. Here is December:

| Station | Sunshine, December |
|---|---:|
| Sevilla/San Pablo ([Seville](/spain/seville/)) | 185.9 hours |
| Larnaka Airport ([Cyprus](/cyprus/)) | 177.1 hours |
| Palma-Puerto ([Mallorca](/spain/mallorca/)) | 157.1 hours |
| Valencia ([Valencia](/spain/valencia/)) | 151.8 hours |
| Nice ([Nice](/france/nice/)) | 145 hours |
| Barcelona/Aeropuerto ([Barcelona](/spain/barcelona/)) | 104.5 hours |

The Roma Fiumicino and Napoli Capodichino files leave the December sunshine row blank, so Rome and Naples are not in this table, and we have no sunshine figure for Lisbon or Athens.

Seville has about 29 more hours of December sun than Palma, which works out to roughly an hour a day, and about 9 more than Larnaka. That, more than the 0.7 °C, is the real difference between the Spanish three: Seville is sunnier and cooler at night; Palma is milder at night and a little cloudier. Barcelona, despite being the driest, has the least sun of the five.

## The bottom of the table

For contrast, the coldest December highs on our European sheets are in the north and east: [Oslo](/norway/) 0.9 °C, [Helsinki](/finland/) 1.4 °C, [Warsaw](/poland/) 2.3 °C, [Stockholm](/sweden/) 2.5 °C and [Kraków](/poland/krakow/) 2.7 °C. [Bucharest](/romania/) averages 4.2 °C by day and −3.3 °C at night in December. [London](/united-kingdom/london/) (8.79 °C) and [Paris](/france/paris/) (8.0 °C) sit in the middle, roughly half Palma's figure.

## Is any of this warm enough?

Honestly, not by beach standards. Our [month lists](/best-places-to-visit-in-december/) use one rule worldwide: an average high of 20–30 °C and under 100 mm of rain. Not one European station on our sheets passes in December, January or February. The best December figure, Larnaka's 18.9 °C, is 1.1 °C short; Palma's 17.5 °C is 2.5 °C short.

What changes if you can move the dates:

- **Go in November instead.** Larnaka averages **23.3 °C** in November with 42.0 mm, and Palma, Valencia and Seville all average **20.1 °C**, with 67.8 mm, 51.6 mm and 72.2 mm of rain. All four pass our rule and appear on the [November list](/best-places-to-visit-in-november/).
- **Or wait for late winter.** Seville's February high is **18.2 °C**, the warmest February on our European sheets (Larnaka is next, at 17.5 °C), and its February rain (46.2 mm) is lower than December's. Valencia's February high is 17.4 °C, slightly above its December figure.
- **January is the low point.** Larnaka (17.1 °C), Valencia (16.7 °C), Mallorca (16.5 °C) and Seville (16.2 °C) are still the warmest European stations we cover, but all four are cooler than in December.

## Before you book a winter-sun trip

The top six stations are in four countries, Cyprus, Spain, Portugal and Greece, and all four use the euro, as do Italy and France. Spain, Portugal and Greece sheets print plug Types C and F at 230 V and 50 Hz; the Italy sheets add Type L. Cyprus is different: Type G, the British three-pin plug, at 240 V, and it drives on the left. See the [plug list](/plug-types-by-country/) and our story on [which countries use the euro](/journal/countries-that-use-the-euro/). The European emergency number 112 is covered in our [112 story](/journal/112-emergency-number/), and if you are wondering what to leave in a Seville tapas bar, our [tipping in Europe](/journal/tipping-in-europe/) story sets out what official sources say.

## What this does not cover

Our ranking only covers places that have a sheet. We have no table for the Canary Islands, Madeira, Malta, Crete or the southern coast of Turkey, so we cannot say how they compare, and we have not guessed. We also have no sea-temperature figures, so this story does not tell you whether you can swim. These are 30-year averages, not a forecast: any one December can be warmer, colder or wetter than the normal.
