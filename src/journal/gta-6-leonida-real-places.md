**Short answer:** Leonida is Florida with the names changed. Grand Theft Auto VI came out on **19 November 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar's own site bills it as "Vice City, USA" and walks through six regions of the state of Leonida. Three of the six map closely onto real Florida places. Two are readings that game coverage widely reports but Rockstar has never stated. One is invented outright, because the real Florida cannot supply it.

Rockstar has published no official real-world mapping of Leonida, and the game's geography is invented. So the table below separates what follows from Rockstar's own names and descriptions from what is reported around the game. We print both, labelled.

## The six regions, and what each one sits on

| Region in the game | What Rockstar says is there | Real Florida match | How firm |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vice City | The big city: Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, a flea market, the port | Miami | Name match. Vice City has been the series' Miami since 2002. |
| Leonida Keys | A tropical archipelago of beach bars and dangerous waters | The Florida Keys | Name match. |
| Grassrivers | Mangrove wetlands, alligators, airboat country | The Everglades | Name match in spirit: grass plus rivers. |
| Port Gellhorn | "Leonida's forgotten coast": faded motels, empty strip malls | Panama City and the Panhandle coast | Reported, not stated by Rockstar. |
| Ambrosia | The heart of the state: a sugar refinery and farmland | The sugar land around Lake Okeechobee, near Clewiston | Reported, not stated by Rockstar. |
| Mount Kalaga | A national landmark on the northern border: hunting, fishing, off-road trails | North Florida forest country, loosely | Reported, and see below. |

## The three you can visit

**Vice City is Miami.** Rockstar names Ocean Beach and Little Cuba inside its city; Miami has Ocean Drive on Miami Beach and Little Havana on the mainland. The neon, the causeways and the cruise port are all real. Our [Miami sheet](/united-states/miami/) covers the practical side of the real city, and our sister site [ThingsMIA](https://thingsmia.com/) walks the real streets the game is based on.

**The Leonida Keys are the Florida Keys.** The real chain runs south-west from Miami to Key West, strung along one road, U.S. Highway 1, the Overseas Highway. It is the only part of the game's map where the real road and the game road do the same job: one way in, one way out, water on both sides.

**Grassrivers is the Everglades.** The name gives it away. Marjory Stoneman Douglas titled her 1947 book on the region *The Everglades: River of Grass*, because the Everglades is a slow, wide river moving through sawgrass, not a swamp standing still. The National Park Service protects about 1.5 million acres of it, and the alligators and airboats Rockstar shows are ordinary sights at the park's edges.

## The one that cannot be real

Mount Kalaga is the tell. Florida is famously flat: its highest natural point is **Britton Hill in Walton County, at 345 feet above sea level**, which VISIT FLORIDA notes is the lowest state high point in the country. A mountain national park is the one piece of geography real Florida cannot offer, so Rockstar invented one and put it up against Leonida's northern border, as far from the peninsula as the map allows. The forests of the real Panhandle are the closest thing it has to stand on.

## What we are not claiming

Only the six region names and Rockstar's descriptions of them come from Rockstar itself. The matches for Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia are the most-reported readings in launch coverage, and coverage does not agree everywhere: Port Gellhorn is also matched to other faded Gulf Coast resort towns. Where a match rests on reporting rather than on the name, the table says so. If Rockstar publishes an official map key, we will update this page and say what changed.

Players who want the real ground under the game can start with our [Miami sheet](/united-states/miami/), and with the [Bahamas sheet](/bahamas/) for the islands that sit just east of the real Vice City, closer to it than anywhere else in Leonida is to anywhere.
